2. EMAIL DELIVERY INVESTIGATION (RESEND ROOT CAUSE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Found why OTP was not reaching test emails:
  * Resend free tier (`onboarding@resend.dev`) strictly blocks sending emails to
    any address other than the Resend account owner (`shitijparmar976@gmail.com`).
  * Other emails (e.g. `bonemann075@gmail.com`) were getting rejected with 403 Forbidden.
